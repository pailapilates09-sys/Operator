Daily close

Finish the daily checks and cash count.

Who: Manager
When: At closing

Open Daily close sheetHelp inside the sheet

Three steps

  1. Use the next empty row from row 5. Keep the ID in column A.
  2. Fill the required fields and choose from the dropdowns. Copy IDs from the existing records.
  3. Read the Check column. Keep the row when finished; update its status rather than deleting it.
What to enter

One row per location per date.
