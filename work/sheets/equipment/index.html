Equipment

Keep equipment checks and next dates together.

Who: Trainer / Manager
When: After an equipment check

Open Equipment sheetHelp inside the sheet

Three steps

  1. Use the next empty row from row 5. Keep the ID in column A.
  2. Fill the required fields and choose from the dropdowns. Copy IDs from the existing records.
  3. Read the Check column. Keep the row when finished; update its status rather than deleting it.
What to enter

One row per equipment item, showing its latest inspection status.

FieldRequiredInstruction
Equipment IDYesKeep the issued ID.
