Attendance

Mark bookings, attendance and no-shows.

Who: Trainer
When: Before and after each class

Open Attendance sheetHelp inside the sheet

Three steps

  1. Use the next empty row from row 5. Keep the ID in column A.
  2. Fill the required fields and choose from the dropdowns. Copy IDs from the existing records.
  3. Read the Check column. Keep the row when finished; update its status rather than deleting it.
What to enter

One row per client per class. Change the status on that row rather than adding a second row.
