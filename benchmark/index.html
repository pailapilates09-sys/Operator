Why Club Pilates is the primary operating-system benchmark

This is a fit-for-purpose selection, not a claim about who delivers the “best class.”

Among the six references, Club Pilates publishes the strongest evidence of a complete repeatable operating system: an operations manual, a general-manager training manual, online intranet access, POS and training resources, recurring memberships, ongoing sales training, recruitment support, site/build support and multi-location scale.

For Paila, that makes Club Pilates the strongest management-system backbone. Reform Body is then used to keep the prototype grounded in the Kathmandu boutique-Pilates market rather than copying a US franchise literally.

1 · Reform Body

LOCAL MARKET BASE

Small classes, Reformer/Mat/Barre/Yoga/private sessions, timetable, memberships, direct booking, beginner-friendly messaging and multiple Kathmandu branches.

Applied: boutique class mix, local schedule-first journey, small-group positioning, first-visit clarity.
