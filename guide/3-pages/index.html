1. Find your way

Learn three screens first: Today → Client journey → Management & decisions.

Start with Today

The top cards give a snapshot; the accountable action queue gives you somewhere to begin. Read the evidence and gate, then follow an action title. The due date belongs to the fictional sample.

Use the navigation

On a large screen, use the sidebar. On a phone, press Menu. Growth & client journey holds leads and client handoffs; Service & membership holds classes, membership and retention; Management & improvement holds decisions and reports.

Pick a guide depth

Use this one-page guide for a reminder, the three-page guide for a first walkthrough, the seven-page guide for a daily/weekly routine and the fifteen-page guide to look up a specific screen.

Recognise the boundary

The site is public presentation with a fictional dataset. It has no private operational login, live shared bookings or customer records. Do not treat the visible prices or terms as an approved offer.

Remember

You can read all tutorials without JavaScript. The interactive workspace requires JavaScript and its static data files.

Try this

Open Today, pick a queue item and say aloud: “This is the issue, this is the evidence, and this role reviews it.”

Open the screens: Today · Client journey · Management & decisions

2. Walk through one fictional client

Trace a completed first visit into a clear next step.

Choose a client

Open Client journey and choose Fictional client 06 in “Inspect client.” The link below selects the same record directly. Read the goal/preference, first/latest completed visit, next action and role.

Read the lifecycle

The funnel is cumulative across the fictional cohort. A person can have a historical second purchase but later exit; active membership is the current state. Funnel cards are not five mutually exclusive piles.

Use filters

Journey stage and Lead source narrow the board. “All stages” and “All sources” show the whole fictional cohort again. The individual inspection selector lets you inspect a client even if the board filter has hidden that row.

Inspect the evidence

The client’s lifecycle table shows dated events. Where a friction or participation signal appears, open Retention and read the stated reason. A signal asks for human review; it is not a diagnosis or certain prediction.

Close the loop carefully

Return to Today and acknowledge one review with “Mark reviewed (demo).” The action becomes “Reviewed in demo.” This is a simulation acknowledgement, not execution of the suggested next step.

Remember

An intro option, a booked introduction, a completed first visit and a second purchase describe different things. Avoid counting them as the same outcome.

Try this

Explain why client 06 still needs a next step after attending the first visit. Then reset the demo.

Open the screens: Inspect client 06 · Retention queue · Action queue

3. Review a decision and reset

Use evidence to propose one bounded experiment.

Read Reports

Choose Daily, Weekly or Last 30 days. Read the displayed event window and the denominator beside a conversion rate. “n/a” means there is no denominator; it is not zero performance.

Read the decision board

Management & decisions gives a fictional constraint, evidence, hypothesis, experiment, owner and review date. Follow the proposed plan and gate before considering any real schedule, staffing or price change.

Try the financial scenario

Change one numeric assumption in the break-even card. Watch contribution, break-even membership and model surplus change. These are scenario outputs, not actual Paila accounts or approved targets.

Check procedures

The SOP master register shows Draft / Planned status. The tutorial teaches the interface; it does not approve a procedure. Open the existing handbook when you need procedure context.

Reset the browser demo

Return to a workspace screen and press Reset fictional demo. Review acknowledgements, unblocked checklist completions, demo bookings, lapse window, planning lens and the demo review log reset. Report tabs, filters and financial inputs are page-local choices.

Remember

Cash receipts are not earned revenue or profit. Financial scenario surplus excludes several real accounting and capital items; the card lists those limits.

Try this

Finish with one sentence: “We propose this experiment, because of this evidence; this role will review it on this date.”

Open the screens: Reports · Management & decisions · SOP register
