1. Start the day with one priority

Use Today as an inbox for accountable reviews.

Read context before numbers

Look at the fictional snapshot date and data label. The dashboard is not a real-time management feed. Current active members, occupied places, human-review signals and awaiting reviews come from the same fictional model.

Pick an action

Start with the accountable action queue. Each row has evidence, a role, a due date and a gate. Click the title to investigate. Pick one useful issue rather than opening every page at once.

Record the right meaning

Mark reviewed (demo) means you looked at the issue in the simulation. The counter decreases and a browser review log appears. It does not approve equipment release, make a sale or notify anyone.

Choose the held-place review and identify the inspection gate.

2. Enquiry to a clear next step

Use Sales, Client journey and Customers as connected views.

Sales shows acquisition

Compare fictional sources by lead count, completed first visits and second purchases. Use the numerator and denominator; a percentage without enough context can mislead.

The journey shows the handoff

Use stage/source filters and Inspect client to find a record. Read goal/preference, responsible role, next action and dated evidence. Customers uses the same underlying fictional people, not a separate live CRM.

Review an appropriate option

After a completed first visit, a role can review an owner-approved next option. The demo shows this intended handoff. There is no payment, offer sending, contact collection or customer editing control.

Follow client 06 from completed first visit to the next proposed handoff.

3. Run the fictional session desk

Use Classes & schedule to inspect places and transitions.

Choose a session

Press a time/session button. Inspect the fictional capacity, available places after holds, occupied places, waitlist, session status and assigned fictional instructor.

Try a transition

A Booked alias in a scheduled session offers Check in, Mark no-show and Cancel demo booking. Check-in is arrival only; it does not create a completed visit or deduct credits. Completed sessions are read-only.

Respect the waitlist

A waitlisted alias can be promoted only when a place is available. Try the 17:30 session: cancel one fictional booking, then promote the waiting alias. An inspection hold never becomes available through a demo control.

Remember

A demo no-show never charges a fee. Booking changes in this tab are not live changes to a customer’s booking.

Try this

Reset the demo, free one place at 17:30, promote the waitlisted alias and confirm occupancy remains within available capacity.

4. Membership and respectful retention

Inspect reasons before suggesting outreach or a policy change.

Read entitlements

Memberships & passes shows fictional options, remaining credits and exceptions. A displayed plan price is an example. A freeze request is still a request; its terms are not approved.

Read explainable signals

Retention shows participation gaps, unused credits and booking friction. These are reasons to investigate the client experience, not a private health profile or predictive risk score.

Try the lapse setting

Enter a whole-number prototype window between 7 and 45 days and press Apply demo window. Other service signals can still appear. Adjusting this setting does not create an approved retention policy.

Respect the member’s choice

Review neutral cancellation reasons and a requested freeze with an authorised person. No automated outreach, charge, refund or membership change happens here.

Inspect client 07’s participation and preferred-time friction, then client 10’s freeze request.

5. Prepare the space and the people

Use role ownership and evidence rather than assumptions.

Read the zone checklist

Operations groups Opening, Midday and Closing tasks. Filter the rhythm. Complete demo check acknowledges an unblocked fictional task; a blocked inspection task has no release control.

Open the preserved layout

Studio Layout is the existing spatial viewer. Checklist area labels are service descriptions; demo class capacities do not establish approved dimensions, equipment count or geometry.

Read coaching evidence

Staff & instructors gives fictional aliases, roles, readiness examples and proposed coaching actions. It does not verify an actual person’s qualification. The proposed checklist uses service consistency rather than popularity ranking.

Keep procedure status visible

Use the SOP master register and handbook for context. Draft or Planned does not mean Approved. Missing approvals stay visible instead of being invented.

Try this

Find one unblocked readiness check and one task that requires an authorised inspection.

6. Weekly evidence to a bounded decision

Read Reports, then Management & decisions.

Choose the right window

Reports offers Daily, Weekly and Last 30 days. New enquiries are events in that window; cohort conversion follows leads acquired in that window through the snapshot date. Active members is the current snapshot state.

Separate cash and scenario results

Recorded cash receipts add fictional purchase amounts. The financial calculator models different assumptions; it is not the cash ledger, earned revenue or real profit.

Name one constraint

Read the preferred-time capacity hypothesis on the decision board. It connects occupied places, waitlist evidence and participation friction to a proposed schedule review. External revenue-stage labels do not classify Paila.

Give the experiment a review

Check owner, experiment, evidence, cost/coverage gate and reassessment date. Marking an action reviewed acknowledges the demo; it does not execute a schedule change.

Try this

Say what evidence would support continuing, adjusting or stopping the proposed experiment.

Open the screens: Reports · Management decisions

7. Know what still needs approval

Use launch and system views to separate planning from operation.

Choose a planning lens

Launch readiness offers Operating review and Pre-opening planning. Selecting a lens does not assert Paila’s actual stage. Workstream states and dependencies are fictional planning examples.

Inspect the source boundary

Systems & data sources distinguishes the fictional seed, browser simulation, disconnected private systems, separate Customer Portal and preserved handbook/layout.

Inspect research application

The research cards show which patterns informed each view. VID-005 is an official workshop-page summary, not a full workshop transcript. External vendor numbers are not Paila targets.

Stop safely

Automations remain inactive candidates. Reset fictional demo clears browser-session simulation choices. Review the live site with the owner before approving policies, integrations or a formal release.

Try this

Explain the difference between a published demo, a private operational system and an approved policy.
